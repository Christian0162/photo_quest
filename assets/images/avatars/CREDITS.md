# Profile avatars

Bundled (not fetched at runtime) so the profile works offline and nothing is
requested from the internet while the app runs.

The pictures are the 3D set from Microsoft's
[Fluent UI Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), released under
the MIT License (see `LICENSE-FluentUI-Emoji.txt`, also shown on the app's
licenses page). Resized to 160px. Grouped into Animals, Travel, Food, Nature
and Fun in `lib/config/constant/app_avatars.dart`.
