# Changelog

All notable changes to Photo Quest are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com), and the app uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - Unreleased

### Added

- Quests for couples, families, friends, pets and solo, with a daily
  "Today's Quest".
- Photobooth with countdown, pose ideas, looks, and Photo, GIF, Boomerang
  and 360° modes.
- Memories kept as journal pages: fanned prints, occasion tags, descriptions
  and dates, filtered by This day, This month or All journey.
- Full-screen photo viewer with swipe, zoom, share and save to photos.
- Keepsakes: decorated strips, grids and polaroids.
- People grouped by relationship.
- Floating "Start a quest" prompt and a three-tab dock with a gliding
  selection bubble.
- App icon, native launch screens and an animated launch reveal.
- Accounts: create an account, confirm your email with a code, log in,
  log out, reset a forgotten password, and add a name and profile photo.
  Your session is remembered. Photos and memories still stay on the phone.
  See `supabase/README.md` for the one-time backend setup.
- Invite a friend to a memory with a short code. Friends enter it under
  Memories, then Shared with you, and can view that one memory (view only).
  Only that memory is uploaded, as smaller copies, and only when you invite.
- Add real friends in People with a friend code. They get a request and
  choose whether to accept. Friends can be invited to a quest or given a memory
  in one tap, with no code to send.
- Do a quest together: invite a friend to a pair or group quest with a code.
  They accept, then see the quest and its memories and can add their own
  photos to them.
- Back up my memories (Settings): off by default, saves a smaller copy of new
  memories on Wi-Fi, with a 100 MB online allowance per person and a meter.
- Remove a memory's online copy to free space.
- Delete your account from the Account screen, with your online photos.
- Animated Photo Quest mark on the welcome and account screens.
